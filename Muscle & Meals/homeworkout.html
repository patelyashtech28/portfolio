Standard Push-Ups

Chest • Front Delts • Triceps

The king of upper-body home exercises. Builds pushing strength and core stability with zero gear.

3–4 sets × 10–20 reps

Intermediate

Pike Push-Ups

Shoulders • Upper Chest • Triceps

Mimics an overhead shoulder press using only your bodyweight to carve broad, strong deltoids.

3 sets × 8–12 reps

Beginner

Reverse Snow Angels

Upper Back • Rear Delts • Posture

Essential for balancing chest work at home. Strengthens your lats, traps, and posture without a pull-up bar.

3 sets × 12–15 reps

Beginner

Chair / Couch Dips

Triceps • Lower Chest

Use any sturdy chair, bed edge, or sofa to isolate and pump up the back of your arms.

3 sets × 12–15 reps

Beginner

Tempo Bodyweight Squats

Quads • Glutes • Core

Lowering slowly for 3 full seconds turns regular air squats into a serious muscle-building stimulus.

4 sets × 15–20 reps

Bulgarian Split Squats

Quads • Glutes • Balance

Rest one foot on your couch behind you. One of the best unilateral leg builders in existence.

3 sets × 10–12 / leg

5-Minute Warm-Up & Cool-Down

Never jump into hard sets cold. Follow these quick 5-minute flows before and after your home workout.

🔥 Pre-Workout Warm-Up (5 Mins)

  • Jumping Jacks (light pace) 60 sec
  • Arm Circles (forward & backward) 45 sec
  • World's Greatest Stretch (each side) 60 sec
  • Bodyweight Good Mornings 15 reps
  • Cat-Cow Spine Mobility 60 sec

❄️ Post-Workout Cool-Down (5 Mins)

  • Doorway Chest & Shoulder Stretch 45 sec / side
  • Child's Pose Deep Breathing 60 sec
  • Standing Quad Stretch 45 sec / leg
  • Seated Hamstring Reach 60 sec
  • Pigeon Glute Stretch 45 sec / side
