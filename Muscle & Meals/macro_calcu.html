Nutrition Basics

How Your Macros Work Together

Understanding calories and macronutrients helps you hit your body composition goals without crash dieting.

4 Calories / Gram

Protein (Muscle Builder)

Repairs muscle fibers broken down during workouts and keeps you fuller for longer during fat loss.

Best Sources: Chicken breast, eggs, fish, Greek yogurt, paneer, tofu, lentils, whey protein.
4 Calories / Gram

Carbohydrates (Gym Fuel)

Stored in your muscles as glycogen—your body's primary energy source for heavy lifting and cardio endurance.

Best Sources: Oats, rice, sweet potatoes, bananas, whole-wheat pasta, quinoa, berries.
9 Calories / Gram

Healthy Fats (Hormone Support)

Essential for hormone production (including testosterone), joint health, and absorbing fat-soluble vitamins.

Best Sources: Peanut/almond butter, avocado, olive oil, salmon, walnuts, whole eggs, chia seeds.
